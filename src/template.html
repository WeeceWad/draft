What decides a match?

Your chosen overall or season ratings build a team score from attack, midfield, defence and goalkeeping. Natural positions give players their best contribution. Home teams get a small advantage, and stronger teams have better chances, but upsets and draws still happen.

Prices paid do not affect match strength. Hiding ratings does not change the simulation. Every team plays each opponent once at home and once away. With an odd number of teams, one team rests each matchday.
